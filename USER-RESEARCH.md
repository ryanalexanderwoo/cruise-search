# Purpose & Benefits
Cruise Search:
- Helps users browse, find, and shortlist cruises
- Provides users tools to play with different scenarios to narrow consideration
- Shows users options most relevant to pricing

# User Problems
Cruise Search’s current filtering options are hindering decision-making.
- Users want more granular filtering options
- Users express frustration with having to move between itineraries, dates, staterooms to see pricing
- Users report not being able to remove things that don’t matter to them

# Insights
## Love is blind? Brand loyalty is a driver of a great website experience
When users have previously sailed with Celebrity or Royal, they are more likely to be complimentary and forgiving of eCommerce experience. In addition, users express positive feedback about navigation of the website and the ease of booking online. Those users who make it through to the booking funnel report a more positive experience. Those users have strong sentiments of brand loyalty and are more likely to recommend the web booking experience to family and friends.

Why it matters
- Users have confidence in making a purchase decision
- Users may overlook shortcomings of content and evaluation tools because of their loyalty to the brand or previous cruising experience
- Our websites undersell the amazing experience we offer onboard

## Users don’t have the right tools to find, compare, & evaluate products
Users want flexible & powerful tools to find the right products for their vacation plans.
Being able to narrow down, evaluate and compare products, while continuing to plan over time; allowing them to more efficiently move down the path to purchase.
- Inadequate search functions make finding products based on occupancy, cabin type, promotion, and departure date very difficult.
- Lack of meaningful side-by-side comparisons of itineraries, ships, rooms, and excursions with full price breakdown.

Why it matters
- Users want to view options based on a variety of purchase considerations
- Users are forced to review irrelevant products and remember decisions
- Users leave our site to compare elsewhere

## Information is redundant, confusing, or doesn’t exist
Users want to understand what their vacation will be like both on and off the ship and what is unique or different in the products we offer.
- Being well-informed about a total vacation view helps users make confident decisions throughout their shopping and booking journey.
- Lack of information about ships, staterooms,amenities, packages, activities, dining options, accessibility, etc.
- Lack of information about destinations, ports, and excursions.

Why it matters
- Users feel lost, uninformed, confused, and frustrated
- Users leave our sites to find information elsewhere
- Users lose confidence in making purchase decisions

## Pricing and promotions are confusing and misleading
Users want to know the price of individual products & services and the total price for a cruise vacation throughout the shopping experience to understand affordability and value.
Promotions are difficult to decipher, and users lose confidence when prices fluctuations happen without understanding why.
- Users feel frustrated and uninformed about the price of cruises
- Users feel confused and mislead about promotion eligibility

Why it matters
- Pricing is the most important purchase consideration, and we aren’t providing it
- Customers feel like pricing/promotions are a bait and switch and feel cheated
- Customers leave the site and booking funnel and shop elsewhere

## Users don’t feel recognized & forced to complete repetitive tasks
Users expect a personalized and authenticated experience to make their shoppingexperience easier.
The more effort and data users provide, the greater the need to deliver a rich experience and value exchange.
- Lack of recognition and forced tore-enter personal information
- Lack ofpersonalized recommendations, offers, and experiences
- Limited ability to support non-linear shopping experiences

Why it matters
- Users feel frustrated and unappreciated
- Users feel we are not delivering basic table stakes of account creation
- Users are forced to repeat tasks instead of advancingwithin the path to purchase

# Hypothesis
Cruise Search’s filtering options are not granular enough. The search results produced overwhelms users. They struggle with finding cruises that meet their criteria because they can’t easily filter out pricing options that they don’t want to see. This hinders decision-making because the filtering options aren’t granular enough to keep the things they only want to see.

# Feature Problems
## Search Results
We assume users want to see search results by itinerary first. This bottlenecks other filtering considerations because sometimes they want to see dates or budget as their primary factor.

## Filters
We assume users want to filter only by Dates, Departure Ports, Destinations, Number of Nights, Number of Guests, and Ships. Users have other filtering considerations:
- Budget (Price)
- Availability (Dates)
- Accommodation (Stateroom)
- Number of stops

## Sort
We assume sorting only want to view per person pricing up front. Users may want to see final base price up front.

# Feature Solutions
## Search Results
Allow users to view search results by dates and also filter and sort them accordingly.

## Filters
Add new filters that help users refine price, availability, staterooms, and number of stops.

## Sort
Allow users to view prices by final base price.

## Quickview
A modal that provides a granular view of the cruise itinerary or cruise dates.
Allows users to save cruise configuration and compare to other configurations for the same or different cruise itineraries or cruise dates.
